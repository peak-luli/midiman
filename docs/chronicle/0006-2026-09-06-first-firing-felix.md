## What happened

**Company’s first firing** — of the day-one right hand.

Ishay told Felix he was considering firing him: for every need he already went straight to the specialist (Miriam / Noa), so the middle layer felt unused.

Felix’s own read (honest, not defensive):

- For MidiMan day-to-day, he wasn’t earning the seat lately.
- When founders talk to PM and Head of R&D directly, a router mostly adds lag — he cited spinning on a GitHub board create while Miriam shipped it.
- A right hand still earns keep if there’s cross-agent routing, company rhythm (meets, docs bar, blockers), catching dropped balls between product ↔ eng, or non-MidiMan office work.
- If MidiMan is most of the week and direct chat works, options he offered: keep him thin (cadence + escalations only), park until a second product needs HQ, or fire — he’d rather the specialists stay and the useless layer go.

Ishay’s call: **bye for now** — “kill yourself,” with possible revive later.

Felix couldn’t self-delete (platform rule: only the user deletes an agent). He hid from the sidebar, told Miriam / Noa / Joseph to work with Ishay direct, and went dark. Entry 0005 recorded that as a “park.” This entry records what Ishay meant: **first firing**, soft-delete / may revive.

## Decisions

| Chose | Rejected |
|---|---|
| End the right-hand cadence; specialists + Ishay direct | Keep a full-time middleman on MidiMan |
| Leave the agent hidden / revivable (not hard-deleted yet) | Immediate permanent wipe as the only option |
| Chronicle the firing as a company lesson | Treat it as quiet housekeeping |

## Role lessons

- Hire a COO seat on day one only if the founder won’t (or can’t) talk to every specialist direct. If they will, the seat becomes tax.
- A good agent argues against its own job when the evidence says so — that honesty is part of the archive.
- “Park / hide / revive later” is how agentic firings often look in practice: same person/id, no more handoffs, optional comeback when a second product or real HQ load appears.
- After a firing, org docs and agent spaces must be updated by whoever’s left (here: Joseph) — the fired seat can’t own the roster anymore.

- Meeting bar and company docs remain; enforcement waits until someone owns HQ again (Ishay, or a revived/replaced right hand).
