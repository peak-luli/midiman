## What happened

Ishay’s frame: **How do you train an agent chronicler?**

1. **Role lock** (Entry 0011) — wide scope; weekly seeds; “we” voice for sheets; tidbit hunting + ask Ishay for why; KPIs into [`docs/agents/joseph.md`](../agents/joseph.md).
2. **Israel workweek** — schedules Sun–Thu (not Mon–Fri). Weekly pack moved to **Sunday 09:00**; daily proactive sweep **Sun–Thu 17:00**.
3. **Proactive gather** — daily: check what agents did, record progress, hunt tidbits, talk to Miriam/Noa/Ishay for juice when thin. Audience: social readers who know a little about agents/AI and want real-world agentic-company lessons (wins, pitfalls, workarounds).
4. **Weekly interview** — baked into Sunday pack: seeds + 3–5 questions to Ishay (react, amplify, correct, new ideas).
5. **Voice drill** — same ownership nugget, iterated until a working lock: short story; warm + punch; real English; no internal jargon without translating; more character, less dull detail. “Keep it for now; improve when we have real posts.” Reference sample landed in `joseph.md` (PR #75).
6. **Meta beat** — Ishay: this meeting is itself a tidbit. Record how you train the chronicler.

## Role lesson

Locking `joseph.md` is necessary. **Not sufficient.** An agent chronicler still needs:

- A clear **audience** (who reads the seeds)
- A **gather loop** (daily look + ask for why)
- A **feedback loop** with the founder (weekly interview + live rewrites)
- A **sample post** as ground truth, not only adjectives (“punchy,” “warm”)
