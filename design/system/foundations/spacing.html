Spacing & radius

Two scales carry the layout. Distance is 6 inside a group, 14 between bar groups and inside a bar or a card, 16 between sidebar sections, 8 and 12 between panels, 10 between list rows. Radius is 3 for a bar, 4 for a chip, 5 for a small control, 6 for a control, 8 for a panel, 12 for a phone card — the radius grows with the thing, and the padding stays on the same six numbers.

Rules. Distance comes from one list — 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16 — and each number means something: 6 is a seam inside a group, 14 is the join between bar groups and the inset of a bar or a card, 16 is the gap between sidebar sections, 8 and 12 stack panels, 10 stacks list rows. Never put an in-between value in. Padding follows the control: 6px 12px for a button, 6px 9px when segments touch, 3px 8px for a small one, 1px 6px for a chip; a panel pads 10 to 12 and the bar pads 8 14. Height is not set directly — every bar control comes to 30px from a fixed 16px line box plus its own padding plus the 1px border, so the arithmetic is what keeps the row level. Radius rises with size: 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 12, and a new element takes the value of the thing it is nearest in height, not the value of the thing next to it.
