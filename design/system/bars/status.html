Status line, readouts and chips

The right-hand end of every transport bar: what the app is doing, where the playhead is, what you are holding, and whether a note just arrived. Everything here is text, not a control — it is dim, small and tabular, and it is the part of the bar that gives way when the window narrows.

12px dim with tabular <b> figures. .hitc is --hit #5fbf7a, .missc is #ffb0b0 — the light red the dark chrome uses for a number, not the hard --miss used on notes.

Rules. Everything on the right of the bar is a readout, not a control: dim, small, and never bordered. Numbers are tabular so a figure changing never moves anything beside it, and a readout that changes on every note (#played) is mono as well. Only the status line shortens — it takes all the slack and truncates with an ellipsis; every other item holds its width, and below laptop width the row wraps rather than clipping one. Amber on #14161a is the one "on": a chip, a key, a toggle all use it. The two exceptions are named — solo is --play blue on white, because a chip that means "only this one" has to be told apart from a chip that means "this one is on".
