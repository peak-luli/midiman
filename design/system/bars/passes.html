Challenge meter and progress bars

What a step wants of you, and how far along you are. The meter is one .slot per required pass — or a single slot for a "last N seconds" challenge — built once and then only mutated, because it repaints forty times a second under a playhead.

Four rules for one component. They differ only in height (5 or 6px), ground (#22262f on a panel, #2a2f3a on a scrim) and fill (--hit for something you earned, --accent for a clock running down). Everything else — 3px radius, overflow:hidden, an <i> whose width is set from JS — is repeated verbatim in all four.

Nothing emits these any more. .passes and its five states are still in learn.css, but no HTML and nothing in src/ sets the class — the meter's .slot replaced them, and took the same three colours with it (--hit for a pass, #7a3a3d and #ffb0b0 for a failed one, --dim for one not reached). They are shown here only so the rules are accounted for: a 600 11px mono pill, 2px 7px, 4px radius, and a dashed border for .pending. Do not use them in new work.

Rules. The meter is the only place a score is shown while you are playing, and it never changes shape while it runs: the slots are built once for the challenge and only their class, their bar width and their number change after that. Slots share the row equally, so the count of passes is read from the number of slots, not from their size. Colour carries the whole state — dull red #7a3a3d while a pass is in progress (border included: that is the current-pass chrome), --hit green the moment it is over target, #7a3a3d again with #ffb0b0 figures if it failed — and the border is what says which, because it is the part visible from a music stand. A finished slot keeps its number and fades its bar to half; it never disappears. Numbers are 700 mono and tabular, so the row does not twitch at 40 repaints a second. Progress bars are all 3px-radius troughs with a JS-set fill: green for something you have earned, amber for a clock.
